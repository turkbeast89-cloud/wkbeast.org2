<analysis>**original_problem_statement:**
The user wants a full-stack application to manage their crypto mining farm.

**PRODUCT REQUIREMENTS:**
-   **Core Functionality:**
    -   Manage customers and their contact information (specifically WhatsApp numbers).
    -   Manage machine types (e.g., L9, L7) and their respective monthly hosting fees.
    -   Assign multiple machines of different types to each customer.
    -   Automatically calculate each customer's total monthly hosting fee.
    -   Track monthly payments (paid/unpaid/paused status).
    -   Dashboard to view total monthly revenue, costs, and profit.
-   **WhatsApp Integration:**
    -   Generate and send monthly payment reminders via WhatsApp.
    -   The app should automatically skip customers who have already paid or are on a prepaid plan.
-   **Data Management:**
    -   Import customer and machine data from an Excel/JSON file.
-   **Deployment & Access:**
    -   The application should be a web app, accessible from both a Mac and an iPhone, with data synced between them.
    -   The main application should be password-protected to prevent unauthorized access.
-   **Customer Portal:**
    -   A separate login for customers.
    -   Customers can view their own machine status (online/offline), hashrate, and temperature.
    -   Customers can view their payment history and maintenance logs.
    -   Display impressive, manually-adjustable overall farm statistics (e.g., 2430 machines online, 10 offline) to build customer confidence.
-   **ViaBTC Integration:**
    -   Integrate with the user's ViaBTC account via API to automatically fetch and display machine status and hashrate in the customer portal. This should support a main account and multiple customer sub-accounts, each with their own API keys. It should also support multiple coin types like LTC and KAS.

**User's preferred language**: English
